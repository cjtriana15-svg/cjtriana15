Solve triangles using the Law of Sines and Law of Cosines.

About This Project

This program is designed to solve triangles when three or more consistent values are provided. It uses mathematical methods including the Law of Sines and the Law of Cosines to determine unknown sides and angles.

Law of Sines Used to determine missing sides and angles.
Law of Cosines Used to solve triangles from side and angle information.
Classification Identifies the triangle by its sides and angles.
How to use: Enter at least three known values. Unknown values can be left blank. Angles must be entered in degrees.
