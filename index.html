About This Project

Enter at least three consistent values to find the missing sides and angles. Some side-side-angle inputs describe two different triangles; in that case, both answers are shown.

Law of Sines Used to determine missing sides and angles.
Law of Cosines Used to solve triangles from side and angle information.
Classification Identifies the triangle by its sides and angles.
How to use: Enter at least three known values, including one side. Leave unknown values blank. Each side must be positive; angles are in degrees between 0° and 180°. Three angles alone cannot determine the size.
